# Pathways to Energy Abundance

*Research date: 2026-09-18. Working definition of "abundance": firm (24/7) clean electricity at roughly **1–2 ¢/kWh wholesale** — about 2–3× below today's US wholesale power.*

*Honesty note: this was compiled from web-index research (company announcements and press coverage), not live verification. Every cost figure below should be re-verified against primary sources before you cite it or act on it. Where a claim is secondhand, it's flagged.*

---

## The shortlist, ranked

**1. Overbuilt next-gen solar + long-duration storage** — the only pathway already delivering sub-3 ¢/kWh electrons at scale, with a decades-long learning curve. Perovskite tandems (LONGi hit 35.5% in July 2026, marching toward a 43% theoretical limit) could cut balance-of-system costs 20–30%. Iron-air storage (Form Energy's <$20/kWh target, 100-hour duration, first commercial builds 2025–2028) turns multi-day intermittency into a ~1–3 ¢/kWh adder. Combined: **firm clean power at 2–5 ¢/kWh in the 2030s**. No physics breakthroughs needed — manufacturing execution and cost validation. Weakness: land, materials, seasonal storage in high latitudes.

**2. Enhanced geothermal (EGS)** — the breakout firm-clean story. Fervo has 500+ days of stable EGS production at Project Red, halved drilling costs, signed a **396 MW PPA with Google (Sept 1, 2026 — the largest EGS PPA ever**, expandable to ~1 GW by 2030), and IPO'd in May 2026 (~$2.2B) against a 50 GW pipeline. Cost trajectory $7,000 → $5,500 → $3,000/kW points to firm power at 4–6 ¢/kWh this decade. Unlike fission: no fuel-supply or waste politics. Unlike fusion: produces power *now*. Eavor's closed-loop plant in Germany (8.2 MW electric + 64 MW heat, Dec 2025) proves the closed-loop concept too. Quaise's millimeter-wave deep drilling ($180M Series B, Aug 2026) is the high-upside call option — currently ~0.6 miles deep, aiming for 5+ km into 400 °C+ rock.

**3. Advanced fission (SMRs/microreactors)** — licensing finally unlocked: TerraPower's Natrium got the **first-ever NRC construction permit for a commercial non-light-water reactor (Mar 4, 2026, ~18-month review)**; 345 MW in Kemmerer, WY, power early 2030s, Meta funding 690 MW. NuScale remains the only certified design. But: FOAK costs $6,000–9,000/kW, and a UC-Boulder analysis puts NuScale *operating* costs alone at ~$93/MWh. The physics is settled; the economic proof is still ahead. TerraPower's build is the test case to watch.

**4. Fusion** — the highest ceiling, the longest wait. 2025–26 was historic: NIF's 11th ignition (7.9 MJ, gain ~3.8; April 2025 record 8.6 MJ), Helion's first private D-T tests at 150M °C + the first-ever fusion plant licenses (Orion, grid target 2028 — widely doubted), CFS assembling SPARC (first plasma 2027; ARC 400 MW Virginia, early 2030s; ~$3B raised), Pacific Fusion's $900M+ tranched raise, TAE's $6B+ merger. Bottleneck is now materials/tritium/plant engineering, not plasma. Grid power: **early–mid 2030s at best**; abundance (~2 ¢/kWh) needs a manufacturing learning curve that hasn't started.

**5. Cross-cutting enablers** — deep/supercritical geothermal drilling (Quaise) and HTS superconducting transmission (already proven inside fusion magnets) raise the ceiling of every pathway above without generating a watt themselves.

## The rest of the field

- **Space-based solar power** — no meaningful orbital demo flown. Skeptical math: ~$500/MWh vs <$50/MWh for terrestrial solar+storage; needs ~10× launch-cost reduction from even optimistic Starship targets. Not a 5–15 year abundance driver.
- **Artificial photosynthesis** — real lab progress (UNIST 11.2% solar-to-H₂, above the 10% commercial benchmark) but no pilots. A 2030s+ *fuels* play, not grid electrons.
- **OTEC** — proven physics (Hawaii, Okinawa plants), no commercial-scale plant, cost curve unbent in 50 years. Island-diesel niche only.
- **Floating offshore wind** (~$200/MWh today) and **airborne wind** (100–200 kW pilots): credible, incremental, not abundance-bending.
- **LENR** — ~30 orgs, ~$52M cumulative funding, but **no independent, multi-week, MW-scale, third-party-audited demo**. Assign ~zero weight in any 5–20 year abundance plan; reassess only if a credible demo appears.
- **The demand forcing function** — US power demand hits records in 2026 (4,270 TWh) and 2027 (4,349 TWh) on AI data centers (EIA); 40% of AI DCs could be power-constrained by 2027 (Gartner). Hyperscalers are de-risking first-of-a-kind builds directly (Google–Fervo, Meta–Natrium, Google–Xcel iron-air), creating a private-procurement fast lane that favors 2–4-year-delivery options (geothermal, SMRs, solar+storage) over fusion and space solar.

## What to watch next (the binary gates)

1. **Form Energy's first commercial iron-air builds (2025–2028)** — does the <$20/kWh target survive contact with the field? This validates or breaks the #1 pathway.
2. **Fervo Cape Station Phase 1 (first 33 MW test power expected Q4 2026, 400 MW in 2028)** — does the cost curve keep falling toward $3,000/kW?
3. **TerraPower Natrium construction** — on time and near budget? That's the fission test case.
4. **Helion's 2028 grid-power attempt and CFS SPARC's 2027–28 campaign** — the two binary fusion gates.
5. **Tandem PV / LONGi pilot lines (2026–2028)** — 25-year stability at manufacturing yield.
